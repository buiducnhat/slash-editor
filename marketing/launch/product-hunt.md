## Description (≤500)

slash-editor gives your React app the Notion block experience: a slash menu, drag handles,
nested reorder, turn-into, comments and real-time collab. It runs on a headless Tiptap v3
core. The UI arrives through a shadcn registry, so the components land in your repo and follow
your Tailwind tokens. Tables, columns, Mermaid, AI via your own model, markdown import/export.
MIT end to end, no paid tier, nothing hosted.

## Maker's first comment

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I'm Nhat. I kept rebuilding the same Notion-style editor in different products. The engine
(ProseMirror/Tiptap) is great and free. The block UX on top usually isn't: slash menu, drag
handles, block comments.

slash-editor is my attempt to make that layer open and ownable:

🧠 Headless core: logic you update from npm
🎨 shadcn registry: markup you own and restyle
🤝 Collab, comments and AI are all adapters, so you bring your own backend
📜 MIT top to bottom

Try the playground (open it in two tabs with `?collab=ph` to see live collab):
https://slasheditor.dev/playground

I'd love to know which block you'd want next.
