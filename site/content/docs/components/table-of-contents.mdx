## How the active row is chosen

While the editor is editable and focused, the active row follows the caret: move into a heading
and its row highlights, and clicking a row moves the caret there and scrolls the heading into
view. Otherwise — a read-only viewer, or an editable view that isn't focused — there is no caret
to follow, so the active row comes from the scroll position: the last heading at or above the top
of the scroll container wins. Pass `scrollContainer` when the editor scrolls inside its own
container instead of the window.

That viewer case — links, checkboxes, and the scroll-driven outline — is spelled out in the
[read-only guide](/docs/guides/read-only).
